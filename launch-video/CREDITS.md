## Music

**Prelude in C♯ minor, Op. 3 No. 2.** Composed and performed by Sergei Rachmaninoff. Victor (Victrola)
78rpm, 1921. Great 78 Project transfer.
<https://archive.org/details/78_prelude-in-c-sharp-minor_sergei-rachmaninoff_gbia0284165a>

- **Recording:** published 1921. Under the US Music Modernization Act, recordings published before 1926
  are in the US public domain as of 2026. archive.org lists no license on the item.
- **Composition:** published 1893, public domain.
- **Used:** 0:00–0:09.4 and 1:33.75–2:17.03 of the recording, plus one chord from about 3:20.5 for the
  end card. Surface noise from the lead-in groove sits under the ending.
- **Caveat:** the US public-domain status above doesn't cover other countries. Check outside the US
  before any commercial release.

Downloaded but not used in the final cut: Chopin, Mazurka in C♯ minor, Op. 63 No. 3, played by
Rachmaninoff (Victor, recorded 1923-12-27).
<https://archive.org/details/78_mazurka-in-c-sharp-minor_sergei-rachmaninoff-chopin_gbia0015772a>
