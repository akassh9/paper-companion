# Paper Companion: hype film (v2)

**Length:** about 62 seconds. **Master:** 3840×2160 at 60 fps. **Share copy:** 1920×1080.
**Rule:** no UI, no hardware. The film has only two materials: 1940s archive film, and pen drawings
on white paper.

## The idea

People have always argued with papers in the margins, alone. The film opens in the archive: libraries,
lamps, pencils, type. Then a pen starts drawing on white paper. It draws pages, marks a passage, writes
a question in the margin. Answers draw themselves from the paper's own sentences, joined back to their
sources by lines. Each feature is a drawing: claims weighed, ideas linked, a number changed and
everything it moves. The drawings speed up into a web of pages joined by visible links (the Xanadu
dream), and then cut to black on the music's last hit. Title.

## Look

- **Paper world:** warm white paper with fiber and soft light falloff. Near-black fountain-pen ink.
  One accent color, the app's purple, for links. Claims use the app's strength colors (green, orange,
  gray) and What If uses its teal, each only as pen ink. Pages and text are abstract: hand-ruled lines
  of words. Only numbers are ever readable, set in New York, the paper's voice.
- **Archive world:** neutral black-and-white prints, 4:3, with grain and gate weave. In the cold open
  they sit in black. Once the paper world arrives, they reappear as prints laid on the paper, and the
  pen draws around them, linking a 1947 reading room to a drawn page.
- **Type:** SF Pro, very few words. White on black in the cold open, ink-dark on paper after.
- **Pace:** cuts land on the chords. It's slow and heavy for 9 seconds, then accelerates with the
  music.

## Music

Sergei Rachmaninoff plays his own Prelude in C♯ minor, Op. 3 No. 2 (Victor, 1921), from the Great 78
Project. The recording is public domain in the US (published before 1926), and so is the composition
(1892).

- **0:00–0:02.6:** needle drop and surface noise only.
- **0:02.6 / 0:04.0 / 0:05.5:** the three tolling chords. Each one is a hard cut.
- **0:09.4:** a splice into the *Agitato* (1:34 in the recording). The build drives every feature beat.
- **0:35:** the fortissimo return of the theme drives the montage of linked pages.
- **About 0:52:** a hard stop on a chord. Cut to black. Crackle.
- **End card:** one soft chord from the coda rings out.
